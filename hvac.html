Common questions

Do I need a new phone?

No. You keep the phone and the number you have now. The local number we give you simply rings your cell like any other call.

Will customers know it's automated?

The text comes from your business name and we write it with you, so it sounds like you. It doesn't pretend to be a person. It just lets the caller know you're on a job and will get back to them shortly, which is what most people want to hear.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. There's no setup fee, and the first 30 days are free, starting when texting goes live. After that, it's $297/month, month to month, only if you choose to continue. If you don't, we turn it off and you owe nothing.

What if I already have a receptionist?

That's fine. The text only goes out when a call isn't answered, so it covers the gaps: lunch breaks, busy lines, two calls at once, and after hours.

What areas do you serve?

We serve HVAC contractors throughout Brevard County, from Titusville to Palm Bay.

How fast can you set it up?

Usually within 2 to 3 weeks, since new business texting numbers need carrier approval. Most of what we need comes from one short call with you: your business name, hours, and how you'd like the text to read.
