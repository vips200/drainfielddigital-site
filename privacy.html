Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Drainfield Digital is a business run by Terry Rossi, a sole proprietor in Florida, doing business as Drainfield Digital ("we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website (drainfielddigital.com), our email, and our business phone and text line.

What we collect

  • Phone and text information. When you call or text our business line, we receive your phone number, the time of the call or text, and the content of any text messages you send us.
  • Email. When you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.
  • Information you give us as a client. If you sign up for our service, we collect your business name, contact details, and business hours, plus how you'd like your text messages to read.
  • Website visits. This site has no sign-up forms, ads, or tracking cookies. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

How we use it

  • To reply to your call, text, or email, including sending one text back when we miss your call.
  • To set up and run the services you ask us for.
  • To keep records needed for billing, security, and the law.

Text messages (SMS)

If you call our business line and we miss your call, we may send you one text message to follow up. We only text numbers that have contacted us first. We don't add you to marketing lists. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. See the SMS section of our Terms & Conditions for details.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We don't sell your information. We only share it with companies that help us run our business, and only so they can provide that service to us:

  • Our phone and texting provider, which carries our calls and text messages.
  • Our email and website hosting providers.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep call, text, and email records only as long as we need them to serve you, run our business, and meet legal requirements. Then we delete them.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving texts from us.
  • Email hello@drainfielddigital.com to ask what information we have about you, or to ask us to correct or delete it.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it on this page and change the date at the top.
