Why the phone isn't ringing as much as it should

When a septic system backs up, the homeowner grabs their phone and searches "septic repair near me." Whoever shows up first with a clean listing and a phone number gets the call. Most septic companies lose that call for simple reasons:

A weak Google listing

Wrong categories, no photos, a handful of reviews, and no mention of drain field or replacement work. Google doesn't know what you do, so it shows someone else.

An old website, or none at all

A site that's hard to read on a phone, with no clear "call now" button. People leave in seconds and call the next company down the list.

Missed calls you never see

Calls come in while you're in a trench. With no tracking, you don't know how many you missed or where they came from.
